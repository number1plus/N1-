NUMBER1PLUS is an experiential Marketing agency from Berlin, fostering collaborations between artists and brands worldwide. Our name joins the gallery projects Number 1 Main Road in Neukölln and COUNCIL+ in Moabit. Those rooms taught us that community isn't built on reach but on return: the people who come back, and bring someone with them next time. Now we do the same for brands. Plus ones welcome.

Tom Esam

Tom Esam is curator and co-founder of Number 1 Main Road and has curated dozens of ambitious exhibitions across Berlin, London, and Rome. With a background in fine art, he has spent over a decade working within Berlin's contemporary art scene. His practice combines curation and production, building exhibitions and installations from concept through to public realisation.


Mike Morlock

Mike Morlock works where brands, culture and people meet. He was one of the first employees at Number26 – the startup that became N26 – and went on to lead marketing and brand for Holvi in the DACH region. As co-founder and director of COUNCIL+, a gallery in Berlin-Moabit, he staged exhibitions, performances, concerts and artist residencies. Since then he has built campaigns and activations for Volkswagen × Hermes, Werder Bremen, 42 Berlin & Wolfsburg and Deutsche Bahn.
